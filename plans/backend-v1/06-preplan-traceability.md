## Claims intentionally revised

Several phrases in the pre-plan sound more measurable than their available data supports. “Unique reach,” “organic attention,” “independent actors,” “effective control,” “information edge,” and “confidence” need explicit operational definitions. The registry replaces hidden certainty with observed counts, relationship evidence, sample scope, and testable proxies. This preserves the hypotheses while making falsification possible.

Wallet PnL and inferred clustering can be computed in code but are not automatically exact. Transfers, missing cost basis, custodial funding, incomplete history and uncertain identity remain part of their result. Similarly, LLM-produced numbers such as cultural recognition or semantic friction are replaced initially by named rubric fields and evidence.

The user explicitly defines Phase 1 as manual entry AND management due diligence, Phase 2 as automatic new-pair analysis and Phase 3 as auto-trading. Phase 1 includes local optional position records and exit proposals under 09, while execution, posting and autonomous discovery remain outside it.

## Integrating later corrections and source material

For each later chapter or correction, create a small source-backed change proposal containing:

1. **Source:** title, URL/file, transcript version/hash, publication date, relevant timestamps and the precise claim. Distinguish literal teaching from our interpretation.
2. **Hypothesis:** what observable mechanism the lesson predicts; applicable game, chain, venue, timeframe and position size; counterexamples that would challenge it.
3. **Measurement:** existing feature reuse or one new feature contract, exact inputs/units/window, quality conditions, code versus semantic boundary and free-source feasibility.
4. **Decision impact:** descriptive-only, advisory, new opportunity check, or a mechanical risk constraint; justify any new required gate.
5. **Evidence plan:** fixtures, labeled examples, prospective outcomes and evaluation method. Do not adopt a rule because it matches one memorable chart.
6. **Migration:** schema/feature/policy version changes, replay comparison, changed verdict explanations, and rollback. Old snapshots stay unchanged.
7. **Implementation packet:** one bounded outcome with files/contracts and acceptance tests, to be executed in the separate implementation task.

Preserve the distinction between learning a useful lesson and establishing a statistically predictive rule. New chapters can improve questions and measurements immediately; they do not retroactively validate existing thresholds.
